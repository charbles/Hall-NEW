Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Hallmark Logic UK Ltd handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us or use our communications services.

Information we collect

When you contact us, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, contact information and the contents of your enquiry. When you use our services, we may process account information, device and operating system details, and call records. Call records may include the originating and destination numbers, time, duration and completion status.

If you connect a third-party service, we may receive information needed to authorise and support that connection.

How we use your information

We use information to respond to enquiries, deliver and support our services, process calls and transactions, maintain security and prevent fraud. We may also use it to diagnose faults and improve our services.

Depending on the activity, we process information to perform a contract or take steps at your request before entering one, to meet a legal obligation, or for legitimate interests such as providing support and keeping our services secure. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to make websites and services work. If we use optional cookies that require consent, we ask for it before setting them. You can also manage cookies in your browser settings.

Who we share information with

We may share information with providers who help us deliver communications, hosting, technical support or other services. Where required or permitted by law, we may also share information with authorities or other authorised parties to comply with legal obligations, protect rights or prevent fraud.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the UK. Where UK data protection law restricts a transfer, we use an applicable transfer mechanism or safeguards required by law.

Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the service, handle enquiries or disputes, and meet legal obligations. The retention period depends on the type of information and why it is held. We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information.

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or delete your information, restrict or object to its use, or receive a portable copy. You can also complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Contact us

To ask a privacy question or exercise your rights, use the contact details on the Hallmark Logic website.
